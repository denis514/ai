# Outreach message templates — ready to copy-paste

> 4-touch sequence + variations + thank-you templates.
> EN + Финский версии.

---

## Sequence overview

```
Touch 1 → connection request (нет pitch)
Touch 2 → value send (2 days after accept)
Touch 3 → question (5 days after T2, if engaged)
Touch 4 → demo invite (7 days after T3, if replied)
```

---

## Touch 1 — Connection request

**Character limit на LinkedIn:** 300 chars в personal note. Используй полностью.

### EN — Generic (для Nordic non-Finnish)

```
Hi {FirstName}, saw your work at {Company} — particularly {specific observation
from their profile}. I'm building an AI Transformation Playbook for
Product/Ops teams. Would love to be connected.
```

**Variables to fill:**
- `{FirstName}` — first name only, не full name
- `{Company}` — current company
- `{specific observation}` — required! Что-то конкретное:
  - "your post about scaling support with AI"
  - "your background bridging product + ML"
  - "the {Company} case study you presented at {Event}"
  - "your recent move from {OldCompany} to {Company}"
  - "your work on {recent product feature mentioned in their posts}"

### EN — для людей activeпостивших про AI

```
Hi {FirstName}, your {recent_AI_post} resonated — особенно про {specific_idea}.
I'm building an AI Transformation Playbook for Product/Ops teams. Would
love to connect.
```

### Finnish — для Finland targets с финским profile

```
Hei {FirstName}, huomasin työsi {Companyssa} — erityisesti {tarkka havainto
profiilistasi}. Rakennan AI-muutoksen operatiivista playbookia Product-
ja Ops-tiimeille. Olisi mukavaa olla yhteyksissä.
```

**Кому writing in Finnish:**
- ✅ Profile на финском
- ✅ Posts на финском
- ✅ Native Finnish (имя + history)
- ❌ Foreign manager в Finland компании (use English)
- ❌ Если ты не сам Finnish-native — пиши English

## Touch 2 — Value send (2 days after connection accepted)

### EN — стандарт

```
Hi {FirstName}, thanks for connecting.

Quick context — I noticed {observation about their team / recent post}.
I built a free AI map for Product/Ops teams covering Operations,
Marketing, Support, Product, Design, Enterprise and eCommerce
transformation:

{atlas_url}

The {direction} direction might resonate with what you're working on.

(No ask — just sharing in case useful.)
```

**Variables:**
- `{atlas_url}` — your live Atlas URL
- `{direction}` — выбери по их role:
  - Head of Product → "Product"
  - Head of Ops → "Operations"
  - eCommerce role → "eCommerce"
  - Head of Marketing → "Marketing"
  - Head of Support → "Customer Support"
  - Head of Design → "Design"
  - Head of Strategy → "Enterprise"

**Critical:** «No ask» line — это THE key. Не drop.

### EN — варинт for AI-active contacts

```
Hi {FirstName}, thanks for connecting.

Saw your {recent_AI_post}. I built an AI Transformation Playbook
for Product/Ops teams covering exactly that concern — feel free to explore
the {direction} direction:

{atlas_url}

Curious to learn from your perspective when you have time.
(No ask — just sharing.)
```

### Finnish — стандарт

```
Hei {FirstName}, kiitos verkostoitumisesta.

Tein ilmaisen AI-kartan Product- ja Ops-tiimeille. Sisältää Operations,
Marketing, Support, Product, Design, Enterprise- ja eCommerce-muutoksen
polut:

{atlas_url}

Suomenkielinen versio on saatavilla — todennäköisesti ainoa
kaupallinen AI-käsikirja suomeksi tällä alalla.

{direction}-polku saattaa resonoida nykyisten haasteidesi kanssa.

(Ei pyyntöä — jaan vain siltä varalta että hyödyllinen.)
```

**Финский moat hook:** «Suomenkielinen versio... ainoa kaupallinen» — main differentiation.

## Touch 3 — Question (5 days after T2, only if engaged)

**Send only if:**
- Они opened T2 message (LinkedIn shows read status)
- Они reacted в any way (like, view profile, etc)
- Они visited Atlas (если есть tracking)

**Don't send if:**
- T2 unread after 5 days → drop
- No engagement signal → drop
- Already replied substantively → skip to T4 conditionally

### EN

```
Hi {FirstName}, curious — how is your team approaching AI strategy
right now?

Most heads-of-product I talk to either roll their own framework or
hire consulting. I'm trying to learn what's actually working in
mid-market companies.

Would love your perspective if you have 5 min over chat.
```

**Tone:** open question, low pressure, signaling research mode.

### Finnish

```
Hei {FirstName}, kiinnostaa — miten tiimisi lähestyy AI-strategiaa
juuri nyt?

Useimmat Product-johtajat joiden kanssa puhun joko rakentavat oman
viitekehyksen tai palkkaavat konsultoinnin. Yritän oppia mitä todella
toimii mid-market-yrityksissä.

Olisi arvokasta kuulla näkökulmasi jos sinulla on 5 min chatille.
```

## Touch 4 — Demo invite (7 days after T3, only if T3 replied)

### EN

```
Hi {FirstName}, based on our chat — would you be open to a 30-min call?

I'd love to show you how the {direction} direction is structured and
learn about your team's process. It's research more than sales — I'm
pre-revenue and trying to learn what's actually useful.

Calendly: {calendly_url}

If timing isn't right, no worries.
```

**Critical principle:** «pre-revenue» — be honest. People help builders
generously when they know it's not transactional.

### Finnish

```
Hei {FirstName}, keskustelumme perusteella — olisitko avoin 30-min puhelulle?

Haluaisin näyttää miten {direction}-polku on rakennettu ja oppia
tiimisi prosessista. Tämä on enemmän tutkimusta kuin myyntiä —
olen pre-revenue ja yritän oppia mitä todella on hyödyllistä.

Calendly: {calendly_url}

Jos ajoitus ei sovi, ei hätää.
```

## Thank you message (after interview)

**Send within 2 hours после interview.**

### EN

```
Hi {FirstName},

Thanks so much for the time today — your insights about
{specific thing they said дословно} were genuinely useful for shaping
the product.

If you want updates as we launch Atlas Pro [or notify when ready] —
happy to keep you in the loop. If you happen to know anyone else
who'd be relevant to chat with, I'd appreciate the intro.

Will share what I'm building with you in 2-3 weeks.

Thanks again,
{Founder name}
```

**Why this works:**
- Shows you listened (specific reference)
- Soft ask for referrals
- Promises follow-up (builds long-term relationship)

### Finnish

```
Hei {FirstName},

Kiitos paljon ajastasi tänään — näkemyksesi {tarkka asia} olivat
todella hyödyllisiä tuotteen muotoilemiseksi.

Jos haluat päivityksiä kun Atlas Pro launchaa — ilolla pidän sinut
mukana. Jos tunnet ketään muuta jonka kanssa olisi relevanttia chatailla,
arvostaisin esittelyä.

Jaan mitä rakennan kanssasi 2-3 viikon kuluttua.

Kiitos vielä,
{Founder name}
```

## Что НЕ писать

❌ **Pitch продукт в Touch 1** — LinkedIn flags as spam.

❌ **Generic "Loved your work!"** — no personalization signal.

❌ **"I'd love to get your input on..."** — selfish frame.

❌ **"Quick question..."** в subject — overused.

❌ **Long messages** — keep under 300 chars в T1, under 500 в T2/T3/T4.

❌ **Emoji в business outreach** (исключение: ✨ для warm signals если match их style).

❌ **Multi-link messages** — distract from one ask.

## Метрики (target benchmarks)

| Metric | Target | Yellow | Red |
|--------|--------|--------|-----|
| T1 → connection accept | 30-50% | 20-30% | <20% |
| T2 → engagement (read + react) | 40-60% | 25-40% | <25% |
| T3 → reply rate | 30-50% | 15-30% | <15% |
| T4 → booking rate | 50-70% | 30-50% | <30% |
| Booking → interview held | 70-90% | 50-70% | <50% |

**Yellow** → review template + personalization
**Red** → fundamental issue (ICP / positioning / messaging)

## Calendly setup рекомендации

**URL pattern:** `calendly.com/{username}/atlas-feedback`

**Settings:**
- Duration: 45 min (flex 30-60)
- Hours: 8am-7pm твоего timezone
- Buffer: 15 min между slots
- Cancellation policy: 4 hours
- Confirmation: manual для первых 20 bookings (catch bad-fit before time wasted)

**Custom questions on Calendly form:**
1. "Что приоритетно обсудить?" (open text)
2. "Какой у тебя текущий AI tool stack?" (helps prep)
3. "Email на который отправить calendar invite" (auto-included)

## Edge cases

### Они отвечают на T1 (не accept, а message)

🎉 Очень хороший сигнал. Skip T2, ask question напрямую.

### Они accept T1 без T2 от вас — и пишут first

🎉 Лучший случай. Drop sequence, engage organic.

### Они accept но T2 ignored after 14 days

Drop. Don't re-engage спустя месяц без новой trigger (например their AI-related post).

### Они отвечают grumpy на T2 ("Why are you messaging me")

```
Sorry for the noise. Won't message again. Best of luck!
```

Drop. Не explain self. Не push.

### Они говорят "send me more info"

```
Sent. The {direction} direction is here: {url}.
Curious if it resonates — when you have 5 min.
```

NOT a long pitch. Brief, value-focused.

### Они говорят "expensive"

```
Fair feedback. Currently exploring what feels right —
mind sharing what would feel reasonable?
```

Don't defend. Listen.

---

## Что NOT включать в outreach

- ❌ Pricing в T1-T2 (raises objections too early)
- ❌ Statistics from product audit (sounds salesy)
- ❌ Comparison к конкурентам (defensive frame)
- ❌ "Limited time offer" (cheesy)
- ❌ Multiple links в одном message
- ❌ Generic AI hype words ("revolutionary", "game-changing")
