## 4-touch sequence

### Touch 1 — Connection request (no pitch)

**Template (EN):**
```
Hi [First name], saw your work at [Company] on [specific observation from their profile].
I'm building an AI Transformation Playbook for Product/Ops teams — would love to be connected.
```

**Personalization required:**
- [specific observation] — must read their profile. Examples:
  - «...your post about scaling support with AI»
  - «...your background bridging product + ML»
  - «...the [Company] case study you presented at [Event]»

**Don't:**
- Generic «I like your work»
- Pitch product в first message
- Send без personalization

**Send rate:** 20-30/day max. LinkedIn flags >30 как spam.

### Touch 2 — Value send (2 days after acceptance)

**Template (EN):**
```
Hi [First name], thanks for connecting.

Quick context — I noticed [observation about their team/recent post].
I built a free AI map for Product/Ops teams covering Operations, Marketing,
Support, Product, Design, Enterprise, and eCommerce transformation:

[atlas.example/]

The [direction matching their role/company] direction might resonate
with what you're working on.

(No ask — just sharing in case useful.)
```

**Critical:** «No ask» line — это ключ. LinkedIn outreach с asks early — гарантированный no-reply.

**Personalization:**
- [direction matching] — выбери из 7 transformation directions исходя из их role:
  - Head of Product → Product direction
  - Head of Ops → Operations direction
  - eCommerce role → eCommerce direction
  - и т.д.

**Goal:** value delivered. No expectation of reply.

### Touch 3 — Question (5 days after Touch 2 — only if Touch 2 received any engagement)

**Send only if:**
- They opened message (LinkedIn shows read status)
- They reacted в any way
- They visited Atlas (если у тебя есть tracking)

**Template (EN):**
```
Hi [First name], curious — how is your team approaching AI strategy right now?

Most heads-of-product I talk to either roll their own framework or hire
consulting. I'm trying to learn what's actually working in mid-market companies.

Would love your perspective if you have 5 min over chat.
```

**Goal:** start conversation. Open-ended question, low-pressure.

**Don't skip Touch 2 → Touch 3** напрямую. Touch 2 builds trust.

### Touch 4 — Demo invite (7 days after Touch 3, only if they replied)

**Template (EN):**
```
Hi [First name], based on our chat — would you be open to a 30-min call?

I'd love to show you how the [specific direction] is structured and learn
about your team's process. It's research more than sales — I'm pre-revenue
and trying to learn what's actually useful.

Calendly: [link]

If timing isn't right, no worries.
```

**Goal:** book demo / interview.

**Critical principle:** «pre-revenue» — be honest. People help builders generously.

## Финский angle (specific для Finland targets)

### Когда использовать финский язык

**Use:**
- Their LinkedIn About is в финском
- They post в финском
- Their company is Finnish и Finnish role (e.g., not foreign manager)

**Don't:**
- Mix English + Finnish внутри одного message
- Use если ты не свободен — выглядит translated. Native quality only.

### Финские templates (если применимо)

**Touch 1 (Finnish):**
```
Hei [First name], huomasin työsi [Companyssa] [tarkka havainto profiilistasi].
Rakennan AI-muutoksen operatiivista playbookia Product- ja Ops-tiimeille —
olisi mukavaa olla yhteyksissä.
```

**Touch 2 (Finnish):**
```
Hei [First name], kiitos verkostoitumisesta.

Tein ilmaisen AI-kartan Product- ja Ops-tiimeille. Sisältää Operations,
Marketing, Support, Product, Design, Enterprise- ja eCommerce-muutoksen polut:

Suomenkielinen versio on saatavilla — todennäköisesti ainoa kaupallinen
AI-käsikirja suomeksi tällä alalla.

(Ei pyyntöä — jaan vain siltä varalta että hyödyllinen.)
```

**Finnish moat hook:** «todennäköisesti ainoa kaupallinen AI-käsikirja suomeksi

## Что НЕ делать

- ❌ Send Touch 2 без acceptance Touch 1 — looks pushy
- ❌ Pitch product в Touch 1 — ban от LinkedIn
- ❌ Send >30 connections в день — flagged as spam
- ❌ Connection accept rate <20% → review templates
- ❌ Use ChatGPT-generated personalization без editing — readable
- ❌ Same template для всех — must personalize observation
- ❌ Follow-up faster than 2-day gap — pushy
- ❌ Stalking — если не реагируют на Touch 1 и 2, drop

### Days 1-3 — Setup phase
- [ ] Sales Navigator account (or free LinkedIn)

### Days 8-14 — Conversation + booking
- [ ] Continue 15-20 Touch 1 per day

### Days 15-21 — Interview-heavy
- [ ] 1-2 interviews per day
- [ ] Update `tasks/pricing-validation.md` after each

## Post-interview thank you

After each interview, send:

```
Hi [First name],

Thanks so much for the time today — your insights about [specific thing they said]
were genuinely useful for shaping the product.

If you want updates as we launch Atlas Pro [or notify when ready] — happy to keep
you in the loop. If you happen to know anyone else who'd be relevant to chat with,
I'd appreciate the intro.

Will share what I'm building with you in 2-3 weeks.

Thanks again,
[Founder name]
```

**Why this works:**
- Shows you listened (specific reference)
- Soft ask for referrals
- Promises follow-up (builds long-term relationship)
