3a. Authentication Methods

We offer two authentication methods:

  • Magic Link (email OTP) — we send a one-time link to your email. No password stored. Email used only for authentication.
  • Google OAuth — you sign in via your Google account. We receive your email address and optionally your name from Google. No Google password or payment data is accessed. Data received from Google is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. We only store the email address in our database. You must confirm consent to our Privacy Policy before being redirected to Google.

4. Data Storage & Transfers

Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) hosted in the EU Central region (Frankfurt, Germany). No personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area (EEA).

Google Analytics data is processed by Google LLC. As of 2024, GA4 uses EU-based data processing with IP anonymisation. Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) apply per Google's DPA.

Vercel (CDN/hosting) is located in the EU where possible. Static assets (no personal data) may be cached globally.

6. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the following rights. We respond within 30 days.

  • Right of access — request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten") — delete your account and all associated data via Settings → Delete account, or by emailing us.
  • Right to data portability — download your data as JSON via Settings → Export data.
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw cookie consent or delete your account at any time.

To exercise your rights: denisantropov@gmail.com

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman: tietosuoja.fi

7. Data Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on all database tables — users can only access their own data
  • No secret API keys in client-side code
  • No passwords stored — authentication via Magic Link (email OTP) or Google OAuth
  • Age verification required at registration: users must confirm they are 16 or older (GDPR Art. 8)
