We implement GA4 Consent Mode v2: analytics storage is denied by default and only enabled after your explicit consent. GA4 is configured with anonymize_ip: true and advertising signals disabled. Withdraw consent anytime via Settings → Cookie Preferences → Reset.

Authentication Methods

  • Magic Link (email OTP) — we send a one-time link to your email. No password stored.
  • Google OAuth — we receive your email from Google. No password or payment data is accessed. Data from Google is governed by Google's Privacy Policy. You must confirm consent before being redirected to Google.

Data Storage & Transfers

Your data is stored in Supabase (PostgreSQL) in the EU Central region (Frankfurt, Germany). No personal data is transferred outside the EEA.

Google Analytics uses EU-based data processing with IP anonymisation. Standard Contractual Clauses (SCCs) apply per Google's DPA.

Vercel (CDN/hosting) is EU-preferred. Static assets may be cached globally.

Your Rights (GDPR)

Under GDPR you have the following rights. We respond within 30 days.

  • Right of access — request a copy of all data we hold about you.
  • Right to rectification — correct inaccurate data.
  • Right to erasure — delete your account via Settings → Delete account, or by emailing us.
  • Right to data portability — download your data as JSON via Settings → Export data.
  • Right to restrict processing — request that we limit how we use your data.
  • Right to object — object to processing based on legitimate interest.
  • Right to withdraw consent — withdraw cookie consent or delete your account at any time.

To exercise your rights: denisantropov@gmail.com
Complaints: Finnish Data Protection Ombudsman — tietosuoja.fi

Data Security

  • All data transmitted over HTTPS (TLS 1.2+)
  • Row Level Security (RLS) on all database tables
  • No secret API keys in client-side code
  • No passwords stored — Magic Link or Google OAuth only
  • Age verification (16+) required at registration (GDPR Art. 8)
